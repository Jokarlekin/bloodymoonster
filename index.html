Bloody Moonster (Even more shortened to BM sometimes) is a +16 urban-fantasy novel inspired by New-York-Times cliché best sellers such as Twilight or The Vampire Diaries!... But let's twist some basic tropes from romance drama, shall we?
You follow the story of Ulysse ("The Narrator"), a shy and introverted vampire, as he witnesses Azenor's ("The Not-Like-Other-Girls Protagonist") love story, torn between the demon Bad Boy and the angel Nice Guy, as she tries to go through college years!

The story is currently in its biweekly publishing phase on Wattpad.com (English/French) and sometimes I’m publishing short stories  as well in case you’re curious!
